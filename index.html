Most families keep their health in a plastic folder, a WhatsApp chat and one person's memory. Swasya gives that person a little peace, and gives everyone else a reason to stop asking where the last report went.

Set up in an evening.

You do the setup once. After that, Swasya does the remembering.

  1. Add the people you look after

    Parents, children, yourself. Each gets a profile with conditions, allergies and the doctors who see them.

  2. Snap reports and prescriptions as they come

    Old ones from the folder, new ones from the lab. Swasya reads the values and dates and files them for you.

  3. Choose who gets reminded

    Doses, refills and the next checkup go to the person who needs them, and you hear back when they're done.

  4. Share a summary when it matters

    Before an appointment, send the doctor one clean page instead of a stack of paper.
